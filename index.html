4. Form 4 Section 1 — emergency rescue

If a suitable real assignment/test from the last year exists, use it. If not, complete this original task yourself. The official Form 4 allows original work for the application.

Original task: A straight line passes through A(2, 3) and B(6, 11). Find its gradient and equation. Find y when x = 9. Decide whether C(10, 19) lies on the line. Draw a labelled sketch and check your equation using A and B.

Field: Functions. Formula Booklet: use the straight-line / gradient formula(s) shown in the official booklet.

  1. Write the problem clearly.
  2. Identify A and B and calculate the gradient using the Formula Booklet.
  3. Use the gradient and one known point to determine the line equation.
  4. Substitute x = 9.
  5. Test C(10,19) by substitution.
  6. Draw a simple labelled sketch.
  7. Substitute A and B back into the equation as a check.
  8. Fill in “Field of mathematics used” and Formula Booklet number(s).

Do not ask AI for the calculations or final answer. You must be able to explain every step.

Important source meanings

  • Tidal flats: sandy beach = spawning; tidal flat = juvenile habitat for about five years.
